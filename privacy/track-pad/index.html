Privacy
Policy.

How Track Pad handles Bluetooth connections, device information, and the input you send to your computer.

Effective date: 19 October 2026 · App: Track Pad · Developer: H2Open
Built to work locally.

Track Pad turns your Android phone into a Bluetooth mouse and keyboard. Its core functionality uses a direct Bluetooth connection between your phone and your selected computer.

01. Information used by the app

To provide Bluetooth mouse and keyboard functionality, Track Pad accesses Bluetooth-related information made available by Android, such as paired-device names, Bluetooth device identifiers, pairing status, and connection status.

This information is used to show available paired devices, establish or restore connections, and display connection status.

02. Mouse and keyboard input

When you use the trackpad or keyboard, the app converts your actions into Bluetooth Human Interface Device (HID) reports sent to the computer you connect to.

These reports may represent pointer movement, clicks, scrolling, or keystrokes.

The app's core HID functionality does not require sending those input reports to an H2Open server.

As with a physical keyboard, the receiving computer and the applications running on it determine how they handle input.

03. Permissions and Bluetooth

Track Pad may request Android Bluetooth permissions needed to discover or list paired devices, connect to a computer, and operate as a Bluetooth input device.

Depending on Android version, system permissions and pairing prompts may differ.

You can manage Bluetooth access and paired devices using your Android device settings.

04. Data storage and sharing

The Bluetooth connection operates directly between your phone and computer.

Track Pad does not require an H2Open account to use its core mouse and keyboard features.

Android and the connected computer may retain Bluetooth pairing records or connection information under their own system settings and privacy practices.

Disconnecting or unpairing a device can be done through the relevant system settings.

05. Third-party services

The core Bluetooth HID features do not depend on an H2Open cloud service.

If a future version introduces advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party services, this policy will be updated to explain the information involved and the relevant providers.

06. Children's privacy

Track Pad is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed for children.

We do not intentionally request personal information from children through the app's core functionality.

07. Security

Bluetooth pairing and connections are handled through Android and the receiving computer's Bluetooth systems.

Use Track Pad only with computers you trust, and remove pairings you no longer need.

08. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change.

The latest version will be published on this page, with an updated effective date where appropriate.
