How Pulse Pad handles Bluetooth connections, device information, keyboard and mouse input, and advertising-related data.

Effective date: 20 October 2026 · App: Pulse Pad · Developer: H2Open
Direct Bluetooth control.

Pulse Pad turns your Android phone into a Bluetooth mouse, trackpad and keyboard. Its core input functionality operates directly between your phone and your paired computer, without requiring an H2Open account or cloud server. The app also integrates Google Mobile Ads, which may process data separately for advertising and related purposes.

01. Information used by the app

To provide Bluetooth mouse and keyboard functionality, Pulse Pad accesses information made available by Android, including:

  • Paired Bluetooth device names.
  • Bluetooth device identifiers.
  • Pairing and connection status.
  • Bluetooth availability and service status.

This information is used to display paired devices, establish and restore connections, and show connection status.

The core Bluetooth functionality does not require uploading this information to an H2Open server.

02. Mouse and keyboard input

When you use Pulse Pad, the app converts touch gestures and keyboard actions into Bluetooth Human Interface Device (HID) reports transmitted to your selected computer.

These reports can represent pointer movement, mouse clicks, scrolling, keyboard characters, and special key commands.

Pulse Pad does not require an H2Open server to process or transmit these input reports. The app's Bluetooth HID functionality does not send the contents of your typing to Google Mobile Ads.

The receiving computer and its applications determine how transmitted input is processed, stored or used.

03. Permissions and Bluetooth

Pulse Pad requests Bluetooth permissions required to access paired devices, establish connections and operate as a Bluetooth input device.

Depending on your Android version, the app may request nearby-device permissions and display system Bluetooth or pairing prompts.

Pulse Pad uses an Android foreground service while providing Bluetooth HID functionality. This allows the connection to continue when the app is in the background.

You can stop this functionality through the Stop Bluetooth Input option in the app's Help & Settings screen. Stopping the service does not require switching off your phone's Bluetooth.

You can also manage Bluetooth permissions and paired devices in Android settings.

04. Advertising and Google AdMob

Pulse Pad integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Advertising is separate from the app's core Bluetooth input functionality.

Google's advertising technology may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android advertising identifier where available and permitted.
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from network information.
  • App interactions and advertising performance information.
  • Diagnostic information, including performance and error data.

Depending on applicable consent choices, settings and regional requirements, advertising may be personalised or non-personalised.

Google processes advertising information according to its applicable policies. For more information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

You can manage certain advertising preferences through your Android or Google account settings. Where required, additional consent choices will be provided through the app.

05. Data storage, sharing and retention

Pulse Pad does not require user registration or an H2Open account to operate its Bluetooth mouse and keyboard features.

Bluetooth input reports are transmitted directly to the paired computer rather than stored on an H2Open server.

Android and the receiving computer may retain pairing records and connection information according to their respective system settings.

Google Mobile Ads may transmit and process advertising-related information using Google's systems. Google determines the applicable retention practices for data processed by its advertising services.

For more information about Google's retention and deletion practices, consult the Google Privacy Policy.

06. Data deletion and user controls

You can stop Pulse Pad's active Bluetooth input service using the Stop Bluetooth Input option within the app.

You can remove Bluetooth pairings through Android and your computer's Bluetooth settings. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored application data according to Android's normal uninstall behaviour.

Advertising-related data processed by Google is subject to Google's privacy controls and applicable deletion procedures.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information controlled by H2Open, contact h2opengames@gmail.com .

07. Third-party services

Pulse Pad uses Google Mobile Ads to provide advertising functionality.

The core Bluetooth HID features do not depend on an H2Open cloud service.

If additional third-party analytics, crash-reporting or other services are introduced in future versions, this policy will be updated to reflect the relevant data practices.

08. Children's privacy

Pulse Pad is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed for children.

We do not intentionally request personal information from children through the app's core Bluetooth functionality.

Advertising-related data processing is subject to Google's applicable policies and any age-related requirements.

09. Security

Bluetooth pairing and communication are handled through Android and the receiving computer's Bluetooth systems.

Use Pulse Pad only with computers you trust and remove pairings you no longer need.

While reasonable precautions are taken to protect app functionality, no electronic communication or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. International data processing

Third-party advertising providers, including Google, may process information on servers located outside your country of residence.

Such processing is governed by the relevant provider's privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Pulse Pad's features, advertising integrations or data practices change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date where appropriate.
