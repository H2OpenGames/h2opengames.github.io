Privacy Policy for Space Battalion

Last updated: 9 September 2026

H2Open (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates Space Battalion. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game.

Information we do not directly collect

Space Battalion does not require you to create an account and does not directly ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

Local game data

Game progress and preferences may be stored locally on your device so that the game can remember your progress and settings.

Advertising and third-party services

Space Battalion uses Google AdMob to provide advertising, including rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and other technical identifiers.

This information may be used for purposes including serving and measuring advertisements, analytics, fraud and abuse prevention, improving advertising services, and personalization where permitted and where appropriate consent or privacy choices have been provided.

For more information about how Google handles information from apps that use its services, see Google's information about partner sites and apps.

Sharing of information

H2Open does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers such as Google in connection with advertising and related services used by Space Battalion.

Consent and privacy choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be provided with consent or privacy choices relating to advertising and the processing of information by advertising providers.

Children

Space Battalion is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

Data retention and deletion

H2Open does not maintain a remote player account or player-profile database for Space Battalion. Locally stored game progress and preferences can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Information processed by third-party providers is retained according to those providers' own policies and applicable law.

Security

We take reasonable steps to use reputable service providers and to limit the information directly collected by Space Battalion. Third-party services are responsible for the security of information they process under their own policies and practices.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game's features, services, or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact H2Open at h2opengames@gmail.com.
