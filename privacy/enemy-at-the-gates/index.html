I. THE OATH OF PRIVACY

Enemy at the Gates ("the Game") is developed and published by H2Open ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you play the Game.

By using the Game, you acknowledge the data practices described in this policy.

II. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Depending on the features and services enabled in the Game, information may include:

  • Gameplay progress, achievements, settings, scores, and preferences.
  • Technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, and diagnostic data.
  • Advertising identifiers and related information processed by advertising providers, where applicable.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us for support.

Some gameplay information may be stored locally on your device. Third-party services may process certain information independently under their own privacy policies.

III. HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the Game.
  • Save gameplay progress and preferences.
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems.
  • Improve gameplay, stability, and performance.
  • Display and measure advertisements, where advertising is enabled.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

IV. ADVERTISING AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The Game may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob, if advertising is enabled. These providers may collect or process device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, and technical information to deliver, measure, or protect ads, subject to applicable law and consent requirements.

Google's privacy information is available at Google Privacy Policy.

You may be able to manage advertising preferences through your device settings or consent choices presented in the Game.

V. DATA SHARING

We do not sell personal information directly. Information may be processed by service providers that support the Game, including advertising and diagnostic providers where integrated.

We may also disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect the security and integrity of the Game and its users.

VI. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Locally stored gameplay information may be removed when you clear the Game's data or uninstall it. Third-party providers may retain information according to their own policies.

VII. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

We take children's privacy seriously. The Game is not intended to collect personal information directly from children without appropriate safeguards. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

Where required, age-appropriate advertising, consent, and data-processing protections will apply.

VIII. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we control, or to object to certain processing.

You can contact us about a privacy request using the details below. For information controlled by third-party providers, you may also need to contact those providers directly.

IX. INTERNATIONAL DATA PROCESSING

Third-party service providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence. Where required, appropriate protections will be applied in accordance with applicable privacy laws.
