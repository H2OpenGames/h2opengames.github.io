  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Interactions with the app and advertisements.
  • Diagnostic and performance information relating to the app and advertising SDK.

These services use information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Processing depends on the SDK configuration, device settings and applicable privacy choices. This does not require precise location permission.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services .

Advertising information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as described in their policies. Their servers may be located outside your country. We may disclose information you provide to us when required by law or necessary to respond to a lawful request.

We do not sell information you send directly to us. This does not mean that third-party advertising involves no sharing or processing of personal information.

5. Your choices

You can manage advertising privacy options through your device and Google account settings. Where a consent or privacy-options form is provided, it allows you to make or change the choices available for your region.

Choosing non-personalised ads does not necessarily stop all data processing; information may still be used to deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud.

Rewarded ads are optional. You can choose not to watch one and continue using the other available gameplay options.

Local game data remains until it is cleared through your device settings or otherwise removed. Backed-up copies are managed by your backup provider. Advertising providers determine their own retention periods under their policies.

Support messages are retained only as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you provided directly to us, or to raise a privacy concern. Available rights depend on applicable law.

We cannot directly erase information held independently by Google. You can use Google's privacy controls or contact Google for those requests.

7. Children and security

We do not ask children to submit personal information through the game. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

8. External links and policy updates

Advertisements and external links may lead to services operated by others. Their privacy policies apply when you use those services.
