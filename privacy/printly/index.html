Privacy at a glance

Printly is designed to discover printers on your local Wi-Fi network and let you choose files or photos for printing. It does not require an account.

1. Overview

Printly ("the App") is provided by H2Open ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed and used when you use the App. Printly helps you discover compatible printers on your local network and prepare documents or images for printing.

2. Information and device permissions

Depending on which features you use, Printly may access:

  • Wi-Fi and local network information: To check network connectivity and discover printers using local network protocols. Discovered printer names, IP addresses and service details may be displayed in the App.
  • Files and documents: Files you explicitly select using your device's file picker, to prepare them for printing.
  • Camera and photos: Photos you choose or capture using your device's camera or photo picker, to prepare them for printing.

These permissions and device features are used to provide the printing functions you request. Printly does not require you to create a user account.

3. Printing and third-party services

Printly prepares selected content for printing and invokes Android's printing interface.

When you confirm a print job, the selected printer and Android print service may receive and process the document or image to complete the job. Their handling of data is subject to their own practices and policies.

Printer discovery communicates with devices on your local network.

The App does not need to upload your documents to an H2Open-operated server to provide its core printing functions.

Advertising and analytics: The version of the project reviewed for this policy did not include an advertising or analytics SDK. If such services are added before release, this policy and the relevant app-store disclosures must be updated.

4. Data storage and security

Files and photos are accessed to prepare the print job. Temporary data may be processed or cached by the App, Android, or the chosen print service as part of printing.

We do not operate a user-account database for Printly's core features.

No method of electronic processing or network communication is completely secure. We recommend using a trusted Wi-Fi network and printers you recognise.

5. Children's privacy

Printly is a general-purpose printing utility and is not designed specifically for children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through user accounts or registration.

6. Your choices

You control which documents and photos you select, whether you use the camera, which printer you choose in Android's print interface, and whether you grant applicable device permissions.

You can manage app permissions in your device settings or uninstall Printly at any time.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Printly's functionality or data practices change.

The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published.

8. Contact us

If you have questions about Printly or this Privacy Policy, contact H2Open at h2opengames@gmail.com .
