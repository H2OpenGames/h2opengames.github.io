1. Welcome to Crappy Bird

Crappy Bird ("the Game") is developed and published by H2Open ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you play the Game.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features and services enabled in the Game, information may include:

  • Gameplay progress, high scores, achievements and game preferences.
  • Device information, operating system, app version and diagnostic information.
  • Advertising identifiers and advertising-related information processed by advertising providers, where applicable.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us for support.

Gameplay information may be stored locally on your device. Third-party services may independently process information under their own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the Game.
  • Save your scores and gameplay preferences.
  • Identify bugs and improve performance.
  • Provide support and respond to questions.
  • Display and measure advertisements, where enabled.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The Game may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob, if advertising is enabled. Advertising providers may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, advertising interactions and technical information.

Such processing may be used to deliver, measure and protect advertisements, subject to applicable consent and privacy requirements.

You can read more in the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising preferences may be managed through device settings or consent options presented in the Game, where available.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not directly sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party providers supporting the Game, such as advertising or diagnostic services where integrated.

We may also disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect the security and integrity of the Game.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Local gameplay data may be removed when you clear the Game's data or uninstall the Game. Third-party providers may retain information under their own retention policies.

7. Children's Privacy

We take children's privacy seriously. The Game is not intended to collect personal information directly from children without appropriate safeguards.

Where required, age-appropriate advertising, consent and data protection measures will apply. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

8. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we control, or to object to certain processing activities.

Contact us using the details below to submit a request. Information controlled by third-party providers may need to be addressed directly with those providers.

9. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers may process information outside your country of residence. Where required, appropriate protections will apply under relevant privacy laws.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, our services or legal requirements change. Any revised policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
