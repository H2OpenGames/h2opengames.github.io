01. Welcome to Crappy Bird

Crappy Bird ("the Game") is developed and published by H2Open ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you play the Game.

02. Information We Collect

Depending on the features and services enabled in the Game, information may include:

  • Gameplay progress, high scores, achievements and preferences.
  • Device information, operating system, app version and diagnostic information.
  • Advertising identifiers and advertising-related information processed by advertising providers, where applicable.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us for support.

Gameplay information may be stored locally on your device. Third-party services may independently process information under their own privacy policies.

03. How Information Is Used

  • To operate and maintain the Game.
  • To save scores and gameplay preferences.
  • To diagnose bugs and improve performance.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To display and measure advertisements, where enabled.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

04. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The Game may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob, if advertising is enabled. Advertising providers may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions and technical information.

This processing may support advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalisation, subject to applicable consent and privacy requirements.

For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising preferences may be managed through device settings or consent options presented in the Game, where available.

05. Sharing of Information

We do not directly sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party providers supporting the Game, including advertising or diagnostic services where integrated.

We may also disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect the Game and its users.

06. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Local gameplay data may be removed when you clear the Game's data or uninstall it. Third-party providers may retain information under their own policies.

07. Children's Privacy

We take children's privacy seriously. The Game is not intended to collect personal information directly from children without appropriate safeguards.

Where required, age-appropriate advertising, consent and data protection measures will apply. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

08. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we control, or to object to certain processing.

Contact us using the details below to submit a request. For information controlled by third-party providers, you may also need to contact those providers directly.

09. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers may process information outside your country of residence. Where required, appropriate safeguards will apply in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the Game, our services or legal requirements. Any revisions will be published here with an updated effective date.
