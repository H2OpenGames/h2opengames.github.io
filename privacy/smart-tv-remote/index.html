Privacy Policy.

A clear explanation of how Smart TV Remote uses your local network, connects to televisions, and handles information.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

✦ Privacy at a glance

Designed to connect your devices.

Smart TV Remote is an application developed by H2Open Games that allows users to discover and control compatible Samsung and Android / Google televisions over a local network. The application uses network connectivity, device discovery, and TV pairing functionality. It may also display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how H2Open Games ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Smart TV Remote mobile application ("the App").

By using the App, you acknowledge the information described in this policy.

02

Information We Process

To provide remote-control functionality, the App may process certain technical information, including:

  • Local network information: Information necessary to identify and communicate with devices on your Wi-Fi network.
  • Television information: Device names, IP addresses, model information, and supported connection protocols.
  • Pairing information: Authentication or pairing information required to establish connections with compatible TVs.
  • Advertising information: Information that may be collected or processed by advertising services integrated into the App.

We do not require you to create an account to use the App.

03

Local Network Discovery

Smart TV Remote searches your local network to identify compatible televisions.

This process may involve local IP address discovery, network requests, and communication with supported TV services.

Discovered device information is used to display available televisions and allow you to select a device for connection.

04

TV Pairing and Remote Control

When connecting to a compatible television, the App may exchange authentication or pairing information with that device.

For example, Android / Google TV devices may require you to enter a pairing code displayed on the television.

Once connected, the App sends commands such as navigation, volume adjustment, media playback, and text input directly to the selected television through the supported connection method.

05

Advertising

The App may display advertisements through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP addresses, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information, depending on device settings and applicable law.

This information may be used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and related services.

You can learn more about Google's data practices here: Google Privacy Policy .

06

Permissions

Depending on your Android version and device, the App may require permissions or system capabilities to:

  • Access network connectivity and communicate with compatible devices.
  • Discover televisions connected to your local network.
  • Maintain and restore connections to supported televisions.
  • Display advertisements through integrated advertising services.

Permissions are used to support the App's functionality and associated services.

07

Data Storage and Retention

The App may store connection or pairing information locally on your device to support reconnection to previously paired televisions.

The handling and retention of information processed by third-party services, including Google AdMob, are governed by those services' applicable policies.

You may be able to remove locally stored App information by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

08

Sharing of Information

Technical information necessary for remote-control functionality is exchanged with compatible televisions on your network.

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as described in their policies.

We do not sell a user account database or require account registration to access the App's remote-control features.

09

Data Security

We aim to use appropriate measures to protect information handled by the App.

Connections to televisions depend on the protocols and security capabilities supported by each device.

No method of electronic communication or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10

Children's Privacy

The App is intended as a general-purpose utility for controlling compatible televisions and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children through account registration.

If you have concerns about information relating to a child, please contact us.

11

Your Choices

You can control certain aspects of how the App and your device handle information.

  • Choose whether to connect to a discovered television.
  • Disconnect from a television using the App.
  • Manage applicable Android permissions through your device settings.
  • Manage advertising preferences through available Android and Google settings.
  • Clear the App's local data or uninstall the App.
12

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, its features, third-party services, or applicable requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.
