Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

This policy describes how Slang Man, published by H2Open Games (“we”, “us”), handles information when you play the game. Slang Man is intended for teens and adults; it is not designed for children under 13.

Information stored on your device

Slang Man stores your completed levels and game progress locally on your device using Flutter SharedPreferences. We do not require an account. Based on the current game code, your progress is not sent to a game account or to our own server. You can erase locally saved progress by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app, subject to your device’s backup settings.

Advertising and information handled by Google

Slang Man uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show interstitial ads and optional rewarded video ads. If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the game uses the ad completion signal to give you the advertised in-game benefit, such as a better hint, letter reveal, or continue option. An interstitial may appear at a break between levels.

When the ads SDK runs, Google may automatically collect and share information such as your IP address, interactions with the app and ads, diagnostic information, and device identifiers including the Android advertising ID where available. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data transmitted by Google’s Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. We do not receive your Google advertising ID in our game code. See Google’s Privacy Policy for information about Google’s handling of data and available controls.

Why this information is used

Local progress lets the game remember which levels you have completed. Ad related information is handled by Google to deliver and measure ads, maintain ad services, and prevent misuse. We do not use the game to request your name, email address, contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone.

Sharing, security, and retention

Google receives information through its advertising SDK as described above. We do not sell your personal information or operate a separate player database for Slang Man. Progress remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app, subject to your device’s backup settings. Google manages retention and deletion of information collected through its services under its own policies. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is protected in transit with TLS.

Your choices

You can decline an optional rewarded ad and continue playing without its reward. Your Android device settings may offer advertising ID and privacy controls. Clearing the app’s data removes locally stored game progress.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. We will update the effective date on this page. If we add accounts, additional analytics, purchases, or other data handling, we will revise this policy and the Play Store Data safety answers as needed.
